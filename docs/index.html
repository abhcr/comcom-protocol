Open protocol · v0.2 experimental

A shared language
for business software.

Imagine booking a dentist appointment from an app you already use. Comcom gives the app and the clinic's software a common way to ask, act, and report what happened.

What, why, and howSee a booking example
Your requestYou→Book a dentist appointment
When both systems support Comcom
A shared request and reply formatYour app⇄Clinic software

The reply tells your app whether the booking was accepted, rejected, or is still pending. Illustrative example, not a live booking service.

What is it?An open set of rules for business software to exchange requests and results. Developers add it to their apps; it is not a booking app or marketplace.
Why does it matter?Connecting different business systems often takes custom work. A shared format aims to make those connections easier to build and their results easier to understand.
How does it work?A business lists the actions it supports. An app reads the instructions, sends a request with permission, and receives a clear result. A form or AI assistant can help a person start.

The exact protocol path

Known website in. Validated outcome out.

  1. 01
    Discover

    GET or HEAD RFC 9727 /.well-known/api-catalog over HTTPS.

  2. 02
    Verify

    Load the manifest and digest-verified profile release bundle.

  3. 03
    Select

    Choose an exact advertised ID, version, direction, kind, and form.

  4. 04
    Validate

    Bind sender, recipient, endpoint, security, extensions, and typed payload.

  5. 05
    Act

    Use the correlated structured result or receipt as the authoritative record.

One authoritative message, three entry paths

The booking command stays the same.

Direct automation

A buyer system discovers the provider, checks availability, and submits the exact booking command.

Generated UI

A conventional form uses schema labels, formats, constraints, and enumerations to produce that command.

AI-assisted

An edge AI gathers intent and confirmation, then submits that same command and explains its structured result.

Natural language can explain the record. It never becomes the authoritative transaction.

Product search → availability → quote → orderAppointment availability → booking → status → cancellationOffer or announcement → accepted, discarded, or duplicate receipt
