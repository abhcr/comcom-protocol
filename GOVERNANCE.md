# Governance

Comcom begins as a maintainer-led open-source specification under the `abhcr` GitHub account. Protocol work, decisions, proposals, and release history are public.

## Decision principles

Maintainers prioritize:

1. deterministic interoperability;
2. a no-AI, low-cost baseline;
3. compatibility and independent implementation;
4. reuse of established standards;
5. small implementable subsets;
6. evidence over hypothetical extensibility;
7. neutrality between vendors, industries, and technology stacks.

## Maintainers

The repository owner is the initial maintainer. Contributors who demonstrate sustained, constructive work across specification, implementation, review, and community support may be invited as maintainers. Maintainer changes are recorded publicly.

## Changes

- Editorial fixes require one maintainer approval and passing CI.
- Compatible capability additions require a public proposal, conformance artifacts, and one maintainer approval.
- Wire-format or incompatible changes require a public proposal, an implementation period, migration guidance, and a new major version.
- Published versioned artifacts are never modified in place.

When independent production adoption warrants it, maintainers will propose a neutral multi-stakeholder governance structure rather than claiming neutrality prematurely.
